2. Why DMD2 (6-Step) Beats Standard 20-Step SDXL by 4.5×

Standard non-distilled SDXL at `CFG 7.0` requires two UNet forward passes per step (positive + negative prompt for classifier-free guidance) across 20 steps = 40 UNet evaluations. By fusing DMD2 into `WAI-Illustrious`, `CFG 1.0` needs only 6 UNet evaluations (50.8s native / 67.6s 1080p), while CFG 1.4 Guided activates negative prompts with 12 evaluations (77.0s 16:9 1080p / 101.0s portrait 1080p) — still 2.3×–4.5× faster than standard SDXL:

5. Bare-Metal PS4 GCN (`gfx701`) Architecture

22 Zero-Scratch OpenCL GCN Kernels (`48.9 KB`)

Compiled with Zig's LLVM AMDGPU backend (`-target amdgcn-amd-amdhsa -mcpu=bonaire`) with zero private scratch spills (`scratch=0`) and 6 SGPRs per kernel. Includes 3 LDS-tiled `Q8_0 × FP16` GEMM kernels (`128×128`, `128×64`, `64×64` using `16.9–17.4 KB` LDS), 5 fused FlashAttention kernels (`attn_d40`, `d64`, `d80`, `d160`, `d512`), `groupnorm_silu`, `geglu`, `im2col_3x3`, `prelu_inplace`, and `upsample2x`.

Direct ("Garlic") Memory Budget: Pro vs. Base PS4

On PS4 Pro (`5120 MiB` direct memory, `4672 MiB` GPU pool), GarlicDiffusion allocates a `493 MiB` NHWC activation arena (supporting up to `1024×1024` SDXL and `32×32` latent VAE tiles). On Base/Slim PS4 (`4096 MiB` direct memory), it automatically switches to a compact `325 MiB` scratch arena (`24×24` VAE tiles), fitting full SDXL in 4,137 MiB and SD 1.5 in 1,577 MiB.
